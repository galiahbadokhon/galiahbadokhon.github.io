Madrasatak Platform

The backend that powers Madrasatak, built with Node.js, Express and PostgreSQL. It ranks schools for each family and runs the parent and school sides of the product.

  • Parent and school accounts with hashed passwords, profiles and avatars
  • Reviews with moderation, favourites, messaging and meeting bookings
  • School dashboard with leads, posts and enrolment confirmation, plus a waitlist and referral codes
